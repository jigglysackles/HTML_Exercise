John Hoffman

Raised an army brat and traveled all over the US and overseas. As an adult, moved to Oklahoma to get my degree, found and married my wife and had 3 kids. Worked for many years in IT going from desktop support up to being a Sr. Engineer overseeing and running dozens of client projects. Eventually, the demands of my job destroyed my health, sanity, and any love I previously held for the work. After being laid off in 2024 I floundered for a bit until I settled on committing to a career change. Tried to self-learn C# through the MS program, but found that I couldn't keep myself engaged and just had too many questions I needed answered that LLMs weren't fulfilling. I decided to get into a more guided course, and I researched many coding groups. Finalizing on TrueCoders because of the Live Instruction courses, which would give me an external source of accountability and human to talk to.
